Join the Chamber

Become a member of the Viña del Mar Chamber of Commerce and gain access to networking opportunities, business promotion, educational events, and exclusive member benefits designed to help your organization grow.

NP Membership

Designed for non-profit organizations. This membership has no annual fee and provides access to chamber meetings, networking events, community resources, and educational workshops.

Bronze Membership

Bronze members receive basic business promotion, inclusion in the Chamber directory, invitations to networking events, and opportunities to connect with local businesses.

Silver Membership

Silver members receive all Bronze benefits plus advertising opportunities, discounted event registration, featured business promotions, and access to exclusive business development seminars.

Gold Membership

Gold members enjoy every Chamber benefit including premium advertising, homepage spotlight opportunities, VIP networking events, sponsorship discounts, leadership opportunities, and priority business referrals.
