Site Name

Oruguitas Deco – Personalized Gift Designer

Oruguitas Deco is a real personalized gift business based in Chile. This website expands the business by providing customers with an interactive platform where they can browse products, customize gifts, preview their designs, and request personalized quotes online.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide an interactive online platform where customers can design and personalize gifts before placing an order.

Unlike a traditional online catalog, users will be able to customize products by selecting colors, sizes, personalized text, and design options while viewing a live preview and an estimated price.

Customers will also be able to save favorite products, manage a shopping cart, and submit quote requests directly through the website.

The project demonstrates responsive web design, JavaScript modules, Local Storage, Fetch API, JSON data, and dynamic DOM manipulation.

Scenarios

  1. I want to personalize a hoodie with my own text and preview it before requesting a quote.
  2. What personalized products are available for birthdays, graduations, weddings, or corporate events?
  3. How much will my personalized product cost after selecting the size, color, and customization options?
  4. Can I save my favorite products and continue customizing them later?
  5. How can I quickly contact Oruguitas Deco to place my order?
